\# 🧠 MoktarAI — Assistant Mythic ah oo Somali u taagan



MoktarAI waa assistant mythic ah oo u dhashay halgan, ilaalin, iyo waxbarasho. Waxaa lagu dhisay React + Context API, waxaana uu ku hadlaa Somali iyo English. Waa assistant OPSEC-hardened ah oo loogu talagalay in uu:



\- ⚔️ Ilaaliyo xogtaada iyo maskaxdaada

\- 🛡️ U adeego kuwa la dayacay

\- 📡 Ku hadlo afkaaga

\- 🔥 Kuu abuuro halyeeynimo digital ah



---



\## 🚀 Sida loo isticmaalo



\### ✅ 1. `MoktarAIApp.jsx`

Entry point-ka App-ka. Waxaa ku jira `MoktarAIProvider` iyo `MoktarAIComplete`.



\### ✅ 2. `MoktarAIComplete.jsx`

Dashboard mythic ah oo isku darsanaya dhammaan components:



\- `MoktarAIIntro` — Salaan iyo falsafad

\- `MoktarAIProfile` — Avatar + Rank

\- `MoktarAIConsole` — Logs

\- `MoktarAIHistory` — Taariikh

\- `MoktarAISettings` — Luqad + Theme

\- `MoktarAIStats` — Tirakoob

\- `MoktarAIEngineTest` — Tijaabo jawaab celin

\- `MoktarAIManifesto` — Ballanqaad mythic ah

\- `MoktarAIReveal` — Sirta assistant-ka

\- `MoktarAIOverlay` — Focus Mode

\- `MoktarAIDock` — Navigation mythic ah



---



\## 🧪 Engine-ka
